Captions on the video

  • The cue being spoken now appears over the video as you play it, wrapped exactly as it will be in the SRT, so you can proofread subtitles the way viewers will see them.
  • Three looks in Settings › Subtitles: Classic (white with a shadow), Boxed (YouTube-style panel) and Outlined (bold with a black outline), with a live sample. View › Captions on Video (⇧⌘C) turns them off and on.
